# Sound and lights stop independently

Starting a scene starts its sound and its lights together, but Stop (in the app, on the Home tile and in the system media controls) ends only the sound: the lit scene keeps showing, and moving, until the lights are stopped on their own, another scene's lights replace them, or a scene without lights is started. We chose this so the app can be used for lights alone; the light movement outliving the sound is deliberate, not a leftover to tie back to playback.
